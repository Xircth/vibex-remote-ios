# VibeX iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-13

VibeX for iOS (`dev.vibex.companion`) is a companion client. It connects only to a VibeX Host that you run (typically on your own computer). It does not create user accounts and does not send app data to VibeX servers.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, payment information, advertising IDs, or analytics. We do not sell or share personal data. We do not track you across apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

- **Pairing credentials** for your Host (kept in the iOS Keychain / local storage on this device).
- **Cached Host data** such as project names, conversations, and messages already visible on your Host, stored only on this device so the app can work offline.

This data stays on the device unless you choose to send it to your own Host. Uninstalling the app, or using in-app “forget Host / clear cache”, removes it.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: used only to scan the Host pairing QR code. Frames are not uploaded.
- **Local network**: used only to reach your Host on the LAN or a tunnel you configure.
